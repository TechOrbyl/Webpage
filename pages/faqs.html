Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it named ORBYL?

The name ORBYL comes from the idea of completing the full orbit of ownership. Every product has a journey — from discovering and purchasing it, to managing invoices, warranties, maintenance, services, and eventually resale. We believe ownership should complete its full orbit before you move on.

How can retailers partner with ORBYL?

Retailers can connect with us to bring a smarter ownership experience to their customers. By partnering with ORBYL, retailers can help customers create digital identities for their products from the moment of purchase. For partnerships, reach out to: retailer@orbyl.co.in

Why do I need ORBYL?

Because ownership shouldn't end at the checkout counter. Important documents get lost, warranties expire unnoticed, and service records become difficult to find. ORBYL helps you keep everything organized, protected, and always within reach, so you can confidently manage the products you own.

When will ORBYL launch?

We're currently building the first version of ORBYL. Join our waitlist to get early access, follow our journey, and be among the first to experience the future of ownership.

What products can I add to ORBYL?

ORBYL is designed for the things that matter most to you — electronics, appliances, vehicles, furniture, watches, and many other products. Our vision is simple: if you own it, it should have its own digital identity.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We believe your ownership data belongs to you. Protecting your information and giving you control over it is one of the core principles behind ORBYL.

Can I join the waitlist today?

Yes. The first 1000 people will get some great perks. Join our waitlist to receive free premium features for 6 months, early access, product updates, and launch announcements. You can also shape the future of ORBYL with your suggestions.

What does “Digital Shadow” mean?

A digital shadow is the record that follows every product throughout its lifetime. It grows with every purchase, warranty update, service, repair, and ownership milestone, preserving the complete story of the things you own.

Is ORBYL just an invoice storage app?

No. Invoices are only the beginning. ORBYL is a digital identity layer for everything you own. It helps you protect, manage, and understand your products throughout their entire lifecycle — from purchase and warranty to maintenance, service history, and future resale. ORBYL is built on the belief that ownership deserves trust, transparency, and a permanent digital shadow.
