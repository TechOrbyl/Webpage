Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026 • Version 1

1. Introduction

At ORBYL, your privacy is central to how we build digital ownership. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information when you use our platform, website, and related services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to create and manage reliable Digital Twins for the products you own.

  • Account informationYour name, email address, and contact details.
  • Product informationInvoices, warranties, manuals, service history, and ownership records.
  • Usage informationHow you use ORBYL to improve product experiences and service quality.

3. How We Use Your Data

“Your data is never the product; it is the key to your product's longevity.”

We utilize collected information strictly for:

Authenticity VerificationReal-time validation of product provenance.
Warranty LifecycleAutomated claim processing and status tracking.

4. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Our systems are continuously reviewed as ORBYL evolves.

5. Your Rights

You remain in control of your ownership information. You can access, update, export, or request deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable legal and retention requirements.

Access DataUpdate RecordsExport DataDelete Account

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Sharing occurs only with essential service providers, such as logistics partners for physical repairs or insurance underwriters for claim processing, and strictly on a need-to-know basis governed by confidentiality agreements.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at connect@orbyl.co.in.
