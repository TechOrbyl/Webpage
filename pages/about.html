Trust Beyond Transactions

Every time you buy something valuable, you believe you're buying ownership. But in reality, you're only buying the product. Everything that proves you own it—the invoice, warranty, insurance, service history, manuals, repair records, even its resale history—immediately begins scattering across emails, paper files, different apps, and your memory. The product stays with you, but its story slowly disappears.

We live in a world where almost everything has evolved into a digital experience. Our money is digital. Our identity is digital. Our conversations, photos, health records, and businesses all have a permanent digital presence. Yet the things we spend our money on every single day still don't have an identity of their own.

If people get to have a digital identity, why don't the things they own? That question became Orbyl.

A Quiet Chaos

A few months after buying a phone, you don't remember how much warranty is left. A year later, finding the invoice feels impossible. Your car's RC, insurance, PUC, and service records live in different places. Appliances need servicing, but you don't remember when. Small problems become expensive because they weren't discovered early enough. When something finally breaks, you're left searching for someone you can trust to fix it. The biggest challenge comes when you decide to sell. The buyer wants confidence: the original invoice, warranty, service history, repairs, ownership details, and proof that the product has been cared for. Most owners cannot provide everything—not because they didn't maintain their product, but because ownership has never had a single place where its history could live.

Every Object Has a Shadow

Orbyl gives every product something it has never had before: a permanent digital shadow. Think of it as the digital shadow of everything you own. Not a copy of the product, but a living identity that grows throughout its lifetime.

Every invoice, warranty, insurance document, RC, PUC, manual, service record, repair, and ownership milestone becomes part of that identity. Everything stays connected to the product instead of being scattered across different places. Orbyl thinks ahead, too. Our AI Ownership Assistant looks at the small problem before it becomes the expensive one—the flicker before the failure, the odd noise before the breakdown. When the problem is bigger than a quick fix, you can book a trusted technician, electrician, or service center straight from the product's own profile. And when the day comes to protect it, or eventually to let it go, Orbyl is already there—connecting you to insurance that fits and building an honest, verifiable ownership history that makes buyers trust you before they've even met you.

Trust The Process

We don't see Orbyl as another app for storing documents. We see it as the digital infrastructure for ownership. A future where every physical product has its own identity. Where products remember everything that happens to them throughout their lifetime. Where ownership becomes transparent, trustworthy, and effortless for consumers, retailers, manufacturers, insurers, service providers, and future buyers alike.

Imagine opening a single app and seeing everything you own not as a list of products, but as a complete portfolio of assets. Every product has its history. Every asset has its value. Every owner has complete visibility.

Possession is nine-tenths of the law. Proof is the rest.

Why We're Building This

Think about how much of life has already gone digital. Money went digital. Conversations went digital. Even you went digital: your identity, your face, your fingerprint, all recognized instantly by the world around you. Ownership never got the same upgrade. It's still stuck exactly where it was decades ago: paper receipts, buried emails, forgotten drawers, and whatever your memory manages to hold onto.

We don't think that's good enough anymore. Every product you buy deserves better than a slow fade into forgotten paperwork. Every owner deserves the peace of mind that comes from knowing exactly what they have, and being able to prove it, always. Trust shouldn't expire the moment you leave the checkout counter. Today, people simply buy products. Tomorrow, every product they buy will carry its own living identity—one that protects its history, preserves its worth, and stays connected to its owner for as long as that ownership lasts.
